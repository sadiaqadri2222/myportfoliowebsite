How I help

Hands-on clinical care, backed by outcome measures and built around what patients can keep doing at home.

Musculoskeletal & sports rehab

Assessment-led plans that get people moving and back to their sport or work.

Neurological & amputee care

Stroke, nerve and limb-loss rehabilitation focused on function and independence.

Manual therapy

Joint mobilisation, dry needling, cupping and taping to reduce pain and restore movement.

Home programmes & education

Simple exercise, posture and ergonomic guidance patients and caregivers can follow.

Community screening camps

Free camps for musculoskeletal, neurological and diabetic-neuropathy patients.

Health research

Study design, data collection and analysis that turn clinic questions into evidence.
